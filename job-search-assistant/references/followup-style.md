# Follow-up style

Based on the user's own sent emails: short, warm, direct, no fluff.

## Rules

- 3 to 5 sentences.
- Same language as the thread (Hebrew thread → Hebrew).
- Greeting with the contact's first name: "Hi <name>," / "היי <name>,".
- Mention the role and the last step (interview date, assignment submitted).
- One clear ask: an update on next steps.
- Close: "Thanks, Maya" / "תודה רבה, מאיה".
- No apologies, no pressure, no exclamation-mark spam (one is fine).
- Never add facts that aren't in the thread (no invented dates, names, or achievements).

## Examples

English, after an interview:

> Hi Dana,
>
> Thanks again for the conversation about the AI Growth Builder role last week. I enjoyed learning about the team and I'm still very excited about the position.
>
> Is there any update on the next steps?
>
> Thanks,
> Maya

Hebrew, after an assignment:

> היי נועה,
>
> רציתי לעדכן שהגשתי את המטלה לתפקיד Fraud Analyst בשבוע שעבר, ולשאול אם יש עדכון לגבי השלב הבא.
>
> אשמח לשמוע,
> תודה רבה, מאיה
